# Global Superstore | Week 2 Data Analysis

# Project overview
This project analyses the Global Superstore dataset using Power BI. The dashboard explores sales performance, profit, customer segments, sales over time, countries and markets, and product sub-categories.
# Objectives
- Summarise overall sales and profitability.
- Explore sales trends over time.
- Compare sales across categories, countries and markets.
- Review profit by region and sub-category.
- Turn findings into practical business recommendations.
# Key dashboard metrics
Metric	Result
Total Sales	12,642,501.91
Total Profit	1,467,457.29
Total Orders	Approximately 25K
Average Sales	246.49
Profit Margin	11.61%

# Key findings
- Sales rose from approximately 2.26M in 2011 to around 4.30M in 2014.
- Technology was the highest-selling category, at approximately 4.74M.
- Consumer customers contributed 6.51M (51.48%) of sales.
- Copiers generated approximately 258,567.55 in profit and Phones approximately 216,717.01.
- Tables showed negative profit and should be investigated.
- The dashboard compares sales by country and market and includes profit by region and sub-category.
# Recommendations
1. Investigate the causes of negative profit in Tables before making investment decisions.
2. Evaluate opportunities in Technology and profitable sub-categories, especially Copiers and Phones.
3. Monitor profit and profit margin alongside sales.
4. Use country and market comparisons to identify local patterns before taking action.
5. Track sales and profit trends over time.
