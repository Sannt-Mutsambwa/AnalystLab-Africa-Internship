# Consumer Complaint Trends & Response Intelligence

## AnalystLab Africa | Analytics Track | Week 3

### Project Overview

This project analyses consumer complaints submitted to the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) concerning Bank of America between May 2017 and August 2023.

The objective is to explore complaint trends, identify patterns across financial products and reported issues, examine company response outcomes, and investigate the time taken by the CFPB to forward complaints to the company.

### Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning, transformation, analysis and dashboard development.
- MySQL: Data validation, querying and analytical investigation.

### Project Scope

The analysis explores:

- Complaint volume and trends over time.
- Complaint distribution across financial products and reported issues.
- Geographic distribution of complaints.
- Company response outcomes and timely response performance.
- CFPB complaint forwarding intervals.
- Consumer complaint submission channels and patterns.

### Key Deliverables

- Cleaned and transformed dataset.
- SQL analysis script.
- Excel dashboard covering four analytical areas.
- Data quality documentation.
- Findings and recommendations.

### Data Source

Consumer complaint data from the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB).

**Analysis period:** 1 May 2017 – 28 August 2023.

### Important Analytical Considerations

- A consumer complaint is not proof of misconduct.
- Complaint volume does not represent complaint rates or establish the likelihood of experiencing a problem.
- CFPB forwarding intervals measure the time between complaint submission and forwarding to the company, not company response or resolution time.
- In-progress complaints are treated separately when calculating timely response performance.
